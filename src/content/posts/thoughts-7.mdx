I Once Killed A Plant

</Quote>


I once killed a plant  
by watering it too much.  
I thought more care  
would help it grow.

Too much of anything  
can quietly become harmful.

Too much love can overwhelm.  
Too much care can suffocate.  
Too much control can break trust.  
Too much attention can lose meaning.  
Too much holding can push away.  
Too much thinking creates confusion.  
Too much comfort stops growth.

I learned it slowly.  
Not everything needs more.

Some things grow better  
with space, not control.

The plant didn't need more.  
It needed me to stop.

That's a harder lesson.  
We rarely understand it in time.

---

More is not always better.  
Sometimes the kindest thing you can do  
is step back  
and let something breathe.
