Notes to Self is exactly what it sounds like things I'm telling myself when life feels heavier than it probably is. No polish, no pretending to have it figured out. Just honest thoughts, written down so future me can come back and remember.

---

<span style="font-size:10rem;color:#db5c55"> 28 </span>

This birthday I stood at Mahakaal, in the early hours, for aarti and darshan.    
There's something about being there, the scale of it, the stillness,   
the simplicity of just showing up and being present,   
that felt like exactly the right way to start 28.

Not too young. Not too old.  
Somewhere in the middle where nobody has a name for it.  
You're past the early twenties chaos,  
but not yet wherever everyone seems to be rushing toward.

It's a strange age to be.  
And I'm starting to think that's exactly the point.

The weight of things is different now.

It's not just about you anymore.  
There are responsibilities that didn't exist a few years ago.  
Family to take care of. Decisions that actually matter.  
Things that used to feel far away are now standing right in front of you.

And somewhere in between all of that,  
people are asking when you're settling down,  
what the plan is,  
what the next box on the list looks like.

The world has a very clear idea of what 28 should look like.  
You're still figuring out what you want it to look like.

And that's okay.  
Even if it doesn't always feel that way.

---

But something has shifted quietly at this age.

I don't need to be noticed anymore.  
I don't need to impress anyone.  
I don't need everyone to like me or understand me or agree with me.

That need, the one that used to hum constantly in the background,  
it's quieter now.  
Not gone. Just quieter.

And in its place, something calmer has moved in.

When I feel sad, I let myself feel sad.  
When I feel confused, I sit with the confusion instead of running from it.  
When I feel behind, I don't spiral, I just look at it.  
Name it. Understand it. Let it pass.

I'm not sure when exactly this happened.  
But somewhere between 27 and 28,  
I started accepting my own thoughts  
instead of fighting them.

That feels like something worth keeping.

---

And one more thing. This one is for anyone reading this who needs to hear it.

When life doesn't go the way you planned, when something you wanted doesn't come through, when a door closes before you even got to knock properly, just give yourself time.

If your intentions are good, if you're not out here hurting people, things have a way of making sense eventually. Not immediately.  
Not always the way you expected. But eventually.

In the moment you might grieve what you lost. You might be angry about it, confused by it. That's okay. Feel all of it.

But later, sometimes much later, you'll understand why it had to happen that way. Why that thing you wanted so badly wasn't actually what you needed. Why the timing was wrong, or the path wasn't yours to take, even when it didn't feel like that at all.

Everything that happens to you is quietly building something you can't see yet.

Just give it time.

---

And as I turn 28, more than anything else, I feel grateful.

Grateful for the life I have.  
Grateful for the people around me.  
Friends who show up. Colleagues who push me forward.  
People who are shaping who I am, directly or indirectly, knowingly or not.

Every conversation, every piece of advice, every moment someone chose to be kind to me, it all adds up to something I can't fully measure but can absolutely feel.

I don't have everything figured out.  
I don't have all the answers.  
But I have good people around me, and I have this life, and right now that feels like more than enough.

Grateful for all of it.  
Every single bit.
