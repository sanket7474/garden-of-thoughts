There's no need to drink the whole sea  
to realise it's salty.  
A sip tells you everything.

But somehow we keep going back.  
As if more will change the taste.  
As if staying longer  
will make it different.

We ignore what we already know,  
hoping it will change  
if we hold on long enough.

But the truth does not change.  
You can try harder,  
you can try a million times over.  
The sea will always be salty.

And sometimes  
knowing early is not the problem,  
walking away is.

### Knowing is easy. Leaving is the hard part.

---

Sometimes we already know.  
We know before we admit it.  
We know before we say it out loud.  
We just don't want to act on it yet.

This could be about a person.  
A job. A habit. A place.  
A version of yourself you've outgrown.

Whatever it is for you,
you probably already know.
